A winning hand: 4 sets + 1 pair

  • Four players around a square table
  • 144 tiles in total
  • Goal: form four sets and a pair
  • Each set is a chow, pung or kong

The tiles

Explore suits, honours, and bonuses

Dots, bamboo, characters

  • Dots (tung) numbered 1-9
  • Bamboo (sok) numbered 1-9, on real sets 1-bamboo is often a bird
  • Characters (maan) numbered 1-9
  • Four copies of each rank

Winds and dragons

  • Winds: East, South, West, North
  • Dragons: Red, Green, White
  • Four copies each
  • Used for specific hands and scoring

Flowers and seasons

  • Eight bonus tiles total
  • Flowers 1 to 4 match East, South, West, North
  • Seasons 1 to 4 match the same seats
  • Set aside when drawn, replace from wall

Game flow overview

From seating to the final discard, the round moves step by step.

Seats, dealer, anticlockwise turns

  • Four seats, each a wind
  • Dealer sits East
  • Play moves anticlockwise: East → South → West → North
  • Dealer starts with 14 tiles and discards first
Table

Wall construction and initial deal

  • Build four walls of 18 stacks (2 tiles each)
  • Roll dice to pick break point
  • Deal 13 tiles each; dealer gets 14th then discards
  • Keep your hand hidden from the others

Break

Draw a tile, then discard

  • Draw from the wall or claim the last discard
  • Check whether your hand now wins
  • Discard one tile face up
  • Continue anticlockwise

Chow, pung, kong claims

  • Chow: three consecutive in same suit, only from left neighbour
  • Pung: three identical, from any player
  • Kong: four identical, exposed or concealed
  • Priority: win > pung/kong > chow when multiple claim

Rounds, dealer stay, draws

  • Four rounds: East, South, West, North
  • Dealer wins: dealer stays East (lin zong)
  • Dealer loses: the deal passes to South
  • Wall runs out, no winner: a draw (lau guk)

Starter strategy tips

  1. 01Choose fast cheap win or build one suit
  2. 02Keep pairs and close tiles, like 3-4 or 6-8
  3. 03Discard lone honours and terminals early
  4. 04Watch opponents' discards for safe tiles
  5. 05Count fan before claiming to meet minimum

Etiquette & house-rule checklist

  • Announce claim loudly (上, 碰, 槓, 食糊)
  • Never touch wall or others' tiles out of turn
  • Shuffle face-down (洗牌 sai paai)
  • Agree on fan minimum and payment method
  • Confirm bonus-tile handling and draw-on-kong rule
  • Decide payment: discarder pays all or everyone pays
